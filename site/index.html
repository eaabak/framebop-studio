How it works

Three steps, from idea to video.

Script, Scenes, Video. Each AI part gets ready with one button, and you see the download size first.

  1. AI draft

    1

    Script

    Write or paste your story, or let AI draft one in your project's language. Nothing goes into your script until you choose it.

  2. 1 2 3

    2

    Scenes

    Split the script into scenes. Each one gets a picture, made with AI or your own, and a voice that reads it aloud. Drag to reorder; edits save on their own.

  3. 3

    Video

    Render a finished MP4 in 1080p or 720p, with slow camera moves, text, captions and music. Every file is checked before it is listed.

Features

Real AI engines, on your own machine.

Framebop installs its own engines once you say yes, each pinned to an exact version. You can start without any AI model at all.

  • Pictures

    Make with AI draws a picture for a scene with Framebop's own stable-diffusion.cpp: about 24 seconds for a 512 × 512 picture on an Apple M4 Pro, with Stable Diffusion 1.5. Add larger, newer models, or upload your own picture.

    stable-diffusion.cpp

  • Voice

    Read with AI narrates each scene with the sherpa-onnx voice engine, with a voice for every interface language: Kokoro for English, Piper for Turkish, German, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. A scene is never cut short of its narration.

    ENTRDEESPT-BR

    sherpa-onnx

  • Script drafts

    Write with AI sets up llama.cpp and Qwen3 4B (2.5 GB) with one consent and drafts a script in your project's language. The draft goes into your script only when you choose.

    llama.cpp · Qwen3 4B

  • Render and check

    Remotion renders the final video in 1080p or 720p (H.264 with AAC sound). FFmpeg then checks every file: length, frame rate, codecs, that every frame decodes and that each narration can be heard where it was placed. The same edit always gives the same file, byte for byte.

    Remotion + FFmpeg

  • Models, your way

    A small, reviewed catalog: every file pinned to its size and SHA-256, with its licence shown before download. Or bring any Hugging Face repository of separate weights (ComfyUI-style or GGUF): you confirm the parts, and only those are downloaded and checked. Downloads resume after a quit.

    Catalog + Hugging Face

  • Video DNA Lab

    Study reference videos with your own YouTube Data API key: public details, comments and a transcript you may use, with findings that each cite the data they rest on. The Story Architect turns a concept into an outline and scenes you edit and approve.

    Research · local language model

Still in the workshop

  • Moving clips Experimental

    Make it move turns a scene's picture into a short clip with Wan 2.2 TI2V 5B. Sampling works on Apple silicon, but decoding the frames is still far too slow to be useful. Uploading your own clips works today.

  • 2D animation Prototype

    A built-in engine for vector or sprite characters with camera moves, drawn in SVG or PixiJS. Demo scenes with test art only, for now.

    Privacy

    Your stories stay on your computer.

    Projects, prompts and media never leave your machine, and once it is set up, Framebop works offline.

    • Everything local

      The app, the AI engines and your library live on your computer. Rendering makes no network requests.

    • No telemetry

      No analytics, no crash reports, no account. This website has no trackers or cookies either.

    • Downloads only with your yes

      Engines and models come from Hugging Face and GitHub only after you agree, with the size shown first. No account or token needed.

    • Checked by SHA-256

      Every engine and model is pinned to an exact version and checked against its published SHA-256 before it is used.

    Besides the downloads you approve, the only part that goes online is Video DNA Lab, and only when you use it: it talks to the YouTube Data API with your own key, kept in your system's secure storage.

    Honest status

    Early, and honest about it.

    Framebop Studio is in alpha. It is developed and tested on macOS with Apple silicon only; Windows and Linux are not tested yet.

    Platforms

    • macOS, Apple silicon Tested Developed and tested here. Framebop's own engines are pinned for it.
    • macOS, Intel Partly The app is expected to work; Framebop's own engines are not yet.
    • Windows 10 and 11 Not yet Designed for; not yet built or tested.
    • Linux Not yet Designed for; not yet built or tested.

    What alpha means here

    • No installer yet. You run Framebop from source, with Node.js, pnpm and Rust.
    • Rendering and the AI helpers work in the development build; the installed app does not contain the renderer yet.
    • Moving clips made with AI are not usable yet, and 2D animation is a prototype.
    • Signed installers for macOS, Windows and Linux are planned.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Is it free?

    Yes. Framebop Studio is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0: no account, no subscription. The AI models you download keep their own licences, shown before each download, and some restrict commercial use. The final render uses Remotion, which is not open source: it is free for individuals, non-profits, companies of up to three people and for evaluation, while larger companies need a Remotion company licence.

    Which computers does it run on?

    Today, Macs with Apple silicon: that is where it is developed and tested, on an M4 Pro with 24 GB of memory. On Intel Macs the app is expected to work, but Framebop's own AI engines are not set up there yet. Windows 10 and 11 and Linux are planned, but not built or tested yet.

    Which AI models does it use?

    For pictures, Stable Diffusion 1.5 through stable-diffusion.cpp, and larger, newer models from the catalog or Hugging Face. For voice, Kokoro and Piper voices through sherpa-onnx. For script drafts, Qwen3 4B through llama.cpp. For moving clips, Wan 2.2 TI2V 5B, which is still experimental. Every model's licence is shown before you download it.

    Do I need the internet?

    Only to download the engines and models you choose, from Hugging Face and GitHub. After that, Make with AI and Read with AI work offline. Video DNA Lab is the one part that goes online, to the YouTube Data API with your own key.

    Can I download it now?

    Not as an installer yet; signed installers are planned. Today you can build it from source on a Mac with Apple silicon, with Node.js, pnpm and Rust: the quick start walks you through it.

    Where are my files kept?

    In one library folder on your computer; on a Mac, ~/Library/Application Support/com.framebop.studio. Imported files are copied, never moved, and finished videos are never overwritten or deleted by the app.
